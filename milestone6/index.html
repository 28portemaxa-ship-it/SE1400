Welcome to my pottery space, a place where I share my love for working with clay and creating by hand. What began as a simple interest has grown into something deeply meaningful, shaping not just pieces but also moments of calm and creativity in my life. I enjoy the process as much as the result, the feel of the clay, the quiet focus, and the excitement of seeing each piece take form. This website is a collection of my journey, my creations, and the inspiration I find in pottery.

I’m still at the beginning of my pottery journey, learning through practice, patience, and plenty of trial and error. Not everything I make turns out the way I expect, but that’s part of what makes it meaningful. Each piece helps me understand the process a little more and build confidence in my skills.
